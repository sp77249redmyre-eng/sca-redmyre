Quote Approvals
This quote will be decided by a vote of 8 committee members.
If 5 or more members approve, the quote will be automatically approved.
If 4 or more members decline, the quote will be automatically declined.
If 3 or more members choose to hold and fewer than 5 approvals are received, the quote will be placed on hold.
Quotes that are on hold or expired can be re-opened for voting by the administrator.
Once a vote has been submitted, it cannot be changed.
