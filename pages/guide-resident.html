A/C Temperature Request
Too hot or too cold? Submit a request and management will adjust the temperature.
🔗 Open this page

If your unit is too hot or too cold, tap the matching button and submit your request. One request is allowed every 30 minutes per tenancy.

❄️ A/C Temperature Request
Too Hot
Lower by 0.5°C
Too Cold
Raise by 0.5°C
StatusMeaning
PendingSubmitted — management will review
ApprovedApproved — queued for adjustment
AdjustingTemperature is being adjusted now
CompletedAdjustment finished
RejectedDeclined — reason provided
⏱️
30-minute cooldown
One request per 30 minutes per tenancy. Each adjustment changes the temperature by 0.5°C.
💬
Message management directly
Tap a request in My Requests to open it — you can send a message to building management right there and see their replies.

Above the Too Hot / Too Cold buttons, you can also see your current A/C setting — the live temperature for your unit, and how close it is to the building's hot/cold limits.
