💡 About This System

Why do we use this?

The Redmyre BMS is a digital platform designed to make building management easier, faster, and more transparent for everyone in the building. Instead of calling, texting, or emailing for every issue — everything is done here.

📢
Stay Informed
Important notices, maintenance updates, and building news are posted here first. You'll never miss an announcement again.
📬
Submit Requests Easily
Need to report a problem? Request a temperature change? It takes less than 2 minutes — and management will be notified instantly.
🔧
Track What's Happening
See all ongoing works in the building so you're never surprised by noise or access restrictions.
🚗
Report Parking Issues
Found a car in your spot? Report it in seconds and all residents are notified immediately.
📞
Emergency Contacts
All building emergency contacts are always available in one place — plumber, electrician, security and more.
🏢
Know Your Neighbours
View occupant details for your unit and the building directory — all in one place.

🔐 Getting Started

How to log in

You'll receive an invitation email from management. Here's what to do next.

1
Check your email
Look for an invitation email from sp77249.redmyre@gmail.com with the subject "You're invited!" — check your spam/junk folder too.
2

Click the blue button in the email. This will take you to the BMS setup page. The link expires in 24 hours.

Choose a password (minimum 8 characters). Use a mix of letters and numbers for security.

Go to sca-redmyre.vercel.app and enter your email and password. Save it to your bookmarks!

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your email.

❓ Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from residents.

I didn't receive my invitation email. What should I do?
Check your spam or junk folder first. If it's not there, contact building management at sp77249.redmyre@gmail.com or call Jacob Kim on 0478 705 406 to resend the invitation.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Go to sca-redmyre.vercel.app and click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email and you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes! The BMS works on all devices — phone, tablet, and computer. For the best experience, add it to your home screen: in your browser, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen".
How do I turn on push notifications?
Once logged in, click the 🔕 OFF button in the top bar and select "Enable Notifications" when prompted. You'll then receive instant alerts for announcements, parking reports, and request updates.
I submitted a request but haven't heard back. What do I do?
Requests are typically reviewed within 1-2 business days. If it's urgent, contact Jacob Kim directly on 0478 705 406.
Some pages I see in this guide are not available to me. Why?
Your access depends on your role (Owner or Tenant/Staff). Some pages are only available to committee members and administrators. This is normal.
Who do I contact for system support?
Contact building manager Jacob Kim: M 0478 705 406 · T 1300 785 007 · sp77249.redmyre@gmail.com
