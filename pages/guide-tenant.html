📱 Install Redmyre BMS on Your Phone

Open the BMS quickly from your phone Home Screen like an app.

TENANT

🍎 iPhone / iPad

1
Open the BMS in Safari.
2
Tap the Share button.
3
Select Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add. The BMS icon will appear on your Home Screen.

🤖 Android

1
Open the BMS in Chrome.
2
Open the Chrome menu.
3
Select Add to Home screen or the install option shown.
4
Confirm and open the BMS from the new Home Screen icon.
Tip: You do not need to download a separate app from the App Store or Google Play.
