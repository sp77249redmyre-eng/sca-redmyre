Quote Approvals
Review and vote on contractor quotes for building works
🔗 Open this page
🔒 Committee & Observer (Strata) only

This is the most important page for committee members. When the building manager obtains quotes, they are submitted here for committee review and voting. You vote to Approve ✅, Decline ❌, or Hold ⏸.

Voting rules (8 committee members total):
✅ 5+ Approve → Approved  |  ❌ 5+ Decline → Declined  |  ⏸ 4+ Hold → On Hold  |  🔀 No vendor reaches 5+ Approve → On Hold (committee re-votes)  |  👥 All 8 voted, no majority → Auto On Hold (re-vote)
