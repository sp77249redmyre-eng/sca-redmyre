How voting works

1
Admin starts voting
You receive an email + push notification that voting has started for a project.
2
Review the quotes
Click on the project to see all vendor quotes, amounts, and attached files.
3
Cast your vote
For each vendor, select ✅ Approve, ❌ Decline, or ⏸ Hold. Add a comment if needed.
4
Result is auto-calculated
The system calculates the result automatically when enough votes are in.

Voting rules — how results are determined

ResultConditionWhat happens
✅ Approved 5 or more Approve votes Quote is approved — admin will proceed with the vendor
❌ Declined 4 or more Decline votes Quote is declined — vendor will not be engaged
⏸ On Hold 3 or more Hold votes, or a tie Quote is put on hold — further discussion needed
⚠️ Conflict Both Approve & Decline thresholds reached Conflict detected — automatically becomes Hold for review
⚠️
Observer (Strata) note — Read only
If your role is Observer (Strata), you can see all voting activity and results but cannot cast a vote. Your vote buttons will not be available.

❓ Help

FAQ — Committee

I received a "Voting Started" email. What do I do?
Go to Quote Approvals, find the project marked "Voting", open it, review each vendor's quote and attached documents, then cast your vote (Approve / Decline / Hold) for each vendor.
Can I change my vote after submitting?
Yes. While voting is still open and a result has not been determined, you can go back into the quote and change your vote. Once the result is confirmed (enough votes reached), voting is closed and votes are locked.
As an Observer (Strata), why can't I vote?
Observer (Strata) accounts have read-only access to the voting system. You can view all quotes and vote tallies but cannot submit a vote. This is by design — only the 8 committee members can cast votes. Voting rules: 5+ Approve = Approved, 4+ Decline = Declined, 3+ Hold (with fewer than 5 approvals) = On Hold.
What happens when there's a conflict in votes?
There are two cases: (1) Conflict — if both Approve (4+) and Decline (4+) thresholds are reached at the same time, the result is automatically set to On Hold. (2) Tie — if multiple vendors share the same highest approval count, the result is set to On Hold for the building manager to decide.
How do I export cost data for the strata meeting?
Go to Cost Analysis, set your date and category filters, then click the "📥 Export Excel" button. The file will download with all currently visible data.
