Quote Approvals

Review contractor quotes and take part in the approval process.

Committee can vote to approve or decline a quote and take part in discussion. Observer (Strata Manager) has read-only access — you can see every quote with full detail, but there is no vote button and you cannot post a decision on a quote.
Quote Approvals

📄 Single quote (one vendor)

A single contractor quote is put up for committee approval.

COMMITTEE

How it works

1
Open the quote to review the project, vendor and amount.
2
Read any attached documents.
3
Cast your vote — Approve or Decline.
4
Once enough votes are in, the quote result is finalised automatically.

📋 Multiple quotes (competing vendors)

Two or more contractor quotes for the same job are compared side by side.

COMMITTEE

How it works

  • Each vendor's quote, price and scope is shown together for comparison.
  • Vote for the vendor you prefer, or decline all of them.
  • The quote with the most votes is selected once voting closes.

📱 Install Redmyre BMS on Your Phone

Open the BMS quickly from your phone Home Screen like an app.

COMMITTEE / OBSERVER

🍎 iPhone / iPad

1
Open the BMS in Safari.
2
Tap the Share button.
3
Select Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add. The BMS icon will appear on your Home Screen.

🤖 Android

1
Open the BMS in Chrome.
2
Open the Chrome menu.
3
Select Add to Home screen or the install option shown.
4
Confirm and open the BMS from the new Home Screen icon.
Tip: You do not need to download a separate app from the App Store or Google Play.
